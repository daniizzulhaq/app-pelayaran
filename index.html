A crewing partner in Indonesia. A personal approach to your people.

PT. Henota Prime Ocean is an Indonesian maritime company focused on seafarer recruitment for domestic and international opportunities. We connect maritime talent with vessel owners and operators, with people and lasting relationships at the centre of our approach.

Based in Bekasi, West Java, our team is a direct point of contact for shipowners, ship managers, and Indonesian seafarers. Talk to us about the scope of support your operation needs.

Ask our team
Who can work with HPO?

We welcome enquiries from shipowners, ship managers, and Indonesian seafarers for domestic and international crewing requirements.

What should I include in a crew request?

Tell us your vessel type, required ranks, operating area, joining timeline, and any specific experience or documentation requirements. Our team will discuss the scope with you.

How do I apply as a seafarer?

Email your updated CV, rank, vessel experience, and availability to our recruitment team. Use the “Send your CV” button in the Careers section to open an application email.

Are there vacancies available now?

Vacancies change with vessel requirements. Contact recruitment for current information. We welcome candidate introductions, but a submitted profile is not a job offer or a guarantee of placement.

Where is HPO based?

Our office is in Bekasi, West Java, Indonesia. Find the current address and a directions link in the contact section. Contact our team before arranging a visit.
