Indonesian people. Maritime connections.

PT. Henota Prime Ocean is an Indonesian maritime company based in Bekasi, West Java. We connect Indonesian seafarers with shipowners and ship managers for domestic and international opportunities, with recruitment and crew administration support shaped around each operation.

Our services

Seafarer recruitment

Candidate coordination, experience review, and recruitment support based on vessel and rank requirements.

Crew management

Crew planning, documentation, scheduling, crew changes, and personnel coordination.

Training & development

Training coordination, competency development, safety awareness, and performance support.

Payroll services

Salary calculations, payment coordination, and payroll documentation based on agreed employment terms.
